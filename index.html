FAQs

Questions about our ghee, purity, and preparation.

What makes Viraasat Farms ghee different?

Viraasat Farms ghee is crafted with focus on purity, rich aroma, danedar texture, and traditional taste. Every jar is made to bring a premium and trustworthy ghee experience to your home.

Is your ghee preservative-free?

Yes, our ghee is made without unnecessary preservatives or artificial additives. We focus on clean preparation and natural richness.

Why does the ghee have a danedar texture?

Danedar texture is a sign of traditional-style ghee preparation. It gives the ghee a rich, grainy feel and enhances the experience of using it in daily meals.

How should I use Viraasat Farms ghee?

You can use it with dal-rice, roti, paratha, khichdi, sweets, cooking, or simply as a daily spoon of nourishment for your family.
